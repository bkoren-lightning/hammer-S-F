Ways to train — weights to sauna

Hammer Strength & Fitness has been Clinton's gym for the people who'd rather lift something heavy than scroll past another ad for one. It's a straightforward setup — no juice bar, no gimmicks — just equipment that works and people who'll spot you if you ask.

The gym is staffed mornings and evenings, and open around the clock to members who've picked up a key. Regulars know each other by name, instructors run classes for every level, and the newest addition — a basketball court — is going in now.
